# Fashion E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview
The Fashion E-Commerce Sales Dashboard is a data analytics project developed to analyse online fashion sales performance using Microsoft Excel. It provides insights into customer demographics, product categories, sales channels, monthly sales trends, geographic performance, and order status.

## Objectives
- Analyse overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing sales channels and product categories.
- Compare sales across gender and age groups.
- Understand monthly and state-wise sales trends.
- Evaluate order status, including deliveries, returns, cancellations, and refunds.
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive visualizations.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel formulas
- Slicers and interactive filters
- Data visualization and business analysis

## Dataset
The dataset contains 31,047 transaction records and 22 columns, including order information, customer demographics, dates, sales channels, product categories, quantities, amounts, order status, and shipping locations.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Delivered Orders
- Sales by Gender
- Sales by Channel
- Sales by Product Category
- State-wise Sales Performance

## Key Business Insights
- Women contribute approximately 64% of the recorded sales value.
- Amazon is the leading sales channel, contributing approximately 35.5% of recorded sales.
- The Set product category contributes approximately ₹1.05 crore in recorded sales.
- Adult customers represent the highest sales contribution among the analysed age groups.
- Maharashtra records the highest state-wise sales in the dataset.
- Order status analysis helps identify opportunities to investigate returns and cancellations.

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for sales and order performance.
- Monthly sales trend analysis.
- Gender-wise and age-group-wise comparisons.
- Sales channel and product category analysis.
- Order status distribution.
- State-wise sales visualization.
- Interactive filters for exploring the data.

## Business Recommendations
- Evaluate channel profitability alongside sales.
- Improve inventory planning for high-demand products.
- Develop targeted marketing strategies for customer segments.
- Investigate the reasons behind returns and cancellations.
- Compare geographic demand and delivery costs to identify growth opportunities.

## Project Workflow
1. Business understanding
2. Data understanding
3. Data cleaning and validation
4. Exploratory data analysis
5. KPI calculation
6. Dashboard development
7. Business insights
8. Recommendations and conclusion

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how sales data can be transformed into meaningful business insights using Excel. The dashboard supports the analysis of sales trends, customer preferences, product performance, and order outcomes.

## Note
Sales figures represent recorded transaction amounts. Profitability, net revenue, and order-level delivery rates require additional validation based on costs, refunds, and order-level data.
